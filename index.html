Real Support on Your Timeline

The trades won't wait for you to figure it out alone.

Apprenticeships are one of the most powerful economic mobility tools we have — but only when people can actually get in, stay in, and finish. Too many fall through the cracks not because they lack the ability, but because the system is fragmented and support arrives too late.

ApprenticeshipBuilder exists to change that. We work at every level of the system — with individuals navigating uncertainty, and with the organizations that shape that system — to make the apprenticeship journey more navigable, more equitable, and more likely to end in certification.

The Apprenticeship Journey

From curious to certified — we're with you at every step.

The journey looks different for everyone. What doesn't change is where the gaps show up — and where the right support makes all the difference.

01

Explore

Not sure if the trades are right for you? That's exactly where we start. Most people don't choose the trades because no one showed them what was actually there.

  • Career awareness and trades exposure
  • Pathway mapping and trade matching
  • Real earning potential and job market data
  • Hands-on VR trades exploration
  • Conversations with working tradespeople
02

Enter

Getting in the door is harder than it should be. The registration process, employer requirements, and program entry points can stop people before they start.

  • Pre-apprenticeship program navigation
  • Employer connection and job readiness
  • Registration and documentation support
  • Financial planning for the early years
  • Foundational skills assessment and upgrading
03

Persist

The middle of an apprenticeship is where most people struggle — and where the system offers the least support. Balancing work, school, and life is genuinely hard.

  • Retention strategies for employers
  • Workplace conflict and advocacy support
  • Mental health and resilience resources
  • Technical period preparation and study support
  • Connection to community and peer networks
04

Complete

Finishing strong means Red Seal certification and a career that's truly yours. The final stretch is critical — and the right preparation changes everything.

  • Interprovincial Red Seal exam preparation
  • Journeyperson status transition planning
  • Continuing education and specialization
  • Career advancement strategy
  • Mentorship and leadership development

About Darrel

The founder who didn't just study the system — he worked inside it.

"I know where apprentices struggle — because I've sat with thousands of them in those exact moments."

Darrel has lived the apprenticeship journey from the inside. As a Red Seal tradesperson, a front-line instructor, and a system-level administrator, he has seen the same gaps appear again and again — not because people lack the drive, but because the system wasn't built to catch them when they stumble.

That experience is what built ApprenticeshipBuilder. Not theory. Not research papers. Real conversations with real apprentices at every stage of the journey — from the ones who made it, and the ones who didn't — and a relentless commitment to making the path better for the next generation of tradespeople.

Darrel brings that same direct, no-nonsense approach to every consulting engagement — whether that's helping a college rethink student support, an employer build a retention program, or a government agency design smarter policy.

Innovation in Trades Exploration

Putting people inside the trades before they choose them.

ApprenticeshipBuilder uses cutting-edge virtual reality technology to bring the trades to life — in classrooms, community centres, career fairs, and remote communities that industrial job sites can't easily reach.

Our VR trades exploration program lets young people, career changers, and community members experience what it actually feels like to work in a skilled trade — before they've committed to a path, and before geography or circumstance has already decided for them.

Because the best way to choose the trades is to feel the work in your hands, even when you're still figuring out where you stand.
